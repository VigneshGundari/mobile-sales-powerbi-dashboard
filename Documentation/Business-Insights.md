# 📌 Business Insights

## Sales Performance

* Compare sales across multiple mobile brands.
* Identify the highest revenue generating brands.

## Regional Analysis

* Analyze city-wise sales performance.
* Identify top-performing locations.

## Payment Analysis

* Compare customer payment preferences.
* Identify the most frequently used payment method.

## Customer Ratings

* Analyze customer satisfaction through ratings.
* Identify rating distribution trends.

## Weekly Sales Trend

* Compare sales across weekdays.
* Identify high-performing and low-performing sales days.

## Business Recommendations

* Focus inventory on top-selling brands.
* Increase promotions in lower-performing regions.
* Encourage digital payment offers.
